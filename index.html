The AI Co-Director for Modern Podcasters

How It Works

Three steps from recording to publish

  1. 01 · Upload & Prompt

    Drop in your multi-mic recordings

    Upload multi-channel audio or video and describe the mood you want, for example: "Fast pace, focus on the guest, strict overlays."

  2. 02 · AI Processing

    AI reads, marks, and cuts

    AI analyzes the transcript context, tags intonation, removes pauses and filler words, and builds the camera switching logic.

  3. 03 · Render & Publish

    Get the finished episode package

    Receive the final video with timecodes, an SEO description, chapters, and cuts ready for Shorts and Reels.
